# Student Performance EDA

Exploratory Data Analysis on Tip dataset from Kaggle.

## What I did

- Analyzed impact of Sex, day, time and total bill on getting tip
- Did visualization on the dataset to showcase distribution, skewness and outliers
- Performed univariate and bivariate analysis across all categorical features

## Key Findings

- Males pays high amount for tip 3.08 than females avg 2.83, but the difference is very small only $0.26
- Day is the strong predictor for tipping, On weekends there a high chance of getting high amount tip
- Time impacts the tip as at dinner time getting tip has more chance than lunch
- Total bill and tip are positively correlated — higher bill, higher tip

## Tools

Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Seaborn
